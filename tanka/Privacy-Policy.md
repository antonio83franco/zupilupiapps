**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Tanka app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Antonio Franco (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

The Application shows fuel and EV charging prices from official public sources near you. The Service Provider never sells your personal data and does not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application collects the following information when you download and use it:

*   **A random user id.** When you first open the Application it creates an anonymous account with a random id, so your settings, favourites and cars can be saved. It contains no name or email.
*   **Your email address**, only if you choose to create an account (or sign in with Apple or Google, where available). It is used to sign you in, to confirm your account and to reset your password.
*   **How you use the Application**, such as the fuel type and filters you choose, the stations you open and favourite, and the navigation app you pick. This is stored with your user id so the Application can remember your preferences.
*   **Vehicle data**, only if you add a car or connect your own OBD-II Bluetooth adapter: make and model, VIN, odometer, fuel level, refuels, maintenance records and fault codes. This is stored on your device, and with your account when you are signed in so it can sync between your devices.
*   **Crash reports.** When the Application crashes or hits an error, it sends a report through Firebase Crashlytics with the error details, the app version, the operating system and the device model. Crash reports carry no user id, email or location.
*   **Your device's Internet Protocol address** (e.g. IP address), which the servers see with every request. It is used to limit abuse (rate limiting) and appears in the hosting provider's standard request logs, which are kept for a short time.

The Application collects your device's location, which helps the Service Provider show the prices around you:

*   **While you use the Application:** your precise location centres the map and finds the nearest stations. Price searches send the area being viewed to the Service Provider's server, which answers the search and does not store your location with your account.
*   **Country detection:** your approximate location sets the country for prices and maintenance rules.
*   **While driving (optional):** only if you turn on automatic mode, give permission and connect an OBD-II adapter, the Application uses your location in the background to suggest the best-priced station ahead when your tank is low.
*   **Place and route search:** when you search for a place or plan a route, the place names and coordinates you search for are sent to the search and routing services listed below.

You can refuse or withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings; you can still search for places and browse prices.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Service Provider may use your email address, if you gave one, to send you important information about your account and required notices. Marketing messages are only sent if you agree to them in the Application, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

**Partner Links**

Some maintenance links and garage offers are partner links. When you open one, the Application records the click with a random reference so the partner can pay a commission. Partners never receive your personal data. Partner content is always labelled "Partner" or "Sponsored".

**Third Party Access**

The Service Provider does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. The Application relies on the following services to work. They process data on the Service Provider's behalf, or as described above, and have their own privacy policies:

*   [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) (servers, database and sign-in)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) (crash reports and feature settings)
*   [Google Maps and Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (map and place search on Android)
*   [Apple Maps](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (map and place search on iOS)
*   [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy) (push notifications for maintenance reminders)
*   [Photon by komoot](https://www.komoot.com/privacy) (place search, when the device's own search finds nothing)
*   [openrouteservice](https://openrouteservice.org/privacy-policy/) (route planning for "Along my route", where available)

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
*   when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
*   with their trusted services providers who work on their behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

**Your Rights**

You can see, export and delete your data at any time:

*   **Export:** Profile → Privacy → Export my data gives you a copy of your data.
*   **Delete:** Profile → Privacy → Delete account and data removes your account and everything stored with it from the servers, and clears the Application on your device.
*   **Consents:** location, background location and marketing are separate choices you can change at any time in the Application or your device settings.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you also have the right to correct your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact the Service Provider at zupilupiapps@gmail.com.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. Uninstalling does not delete data already stored with your account; use Delete account and data first, or contact the Service Provider.

**Data Retention Policy**

The Service Provider keeps your account data for as long as you use the Application. Deleting your account in the Application removes it immediately. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Partner click records are kept for accounting and carry no personal data once your account is deleted. If you'd like the Service Provider to delete your data, please contact them at zupilupiapps@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.
