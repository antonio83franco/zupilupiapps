**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Tanka app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Antonio Franco (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

The Service Provider is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact for anything about your data: zupilupiapps@gmail.com.

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application shows fuel and EV charging prices from official public sources. To do that, it processes the following information:

*   **Location.** Your device's location is used while you use the Application, to show prices near you. If you turn on automatic mode and connect an OBD adapter, the Application also uses your location while you drive, to find the best-priced station ahead when your tank is low. Location is not stored on the Service Provider's servers and is not used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy.
*   **App identifier.** The Application signs you in anonymously with a random identifier, so it can load prices and keep your settings. No name or email is needed. A guest identifier that is not used for 6 months is deleted.
*   **Account (optional).** If you create an account, your email address is stored so you can sign in and sync your favourites, cars and reminders across devices.
*   **How you use the Application.** Searches, filters, the stations you open and the navigation app you choose are used to learn your preferences (for example your usual fuel). This log contains no location and is deleted after 12 months.
*   **Vehicle data (optional).** If you connect a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, the Application reads your car's VIN, odometer, fuel level and fault codes to warn you before you run low and to track maintenance.
*   **Crash reports.** When the Application crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, app version, device model and operating system is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Crash reports contain no user identifier and no location. You can turn them off in Profile → Privacy.
*   **IP address.** The servers see your device's IP address with every request. It is used to limit abuse (rate limiting) and appears in the hosting provider's request logs, which are kept for a short time.
*   **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, a push token is stored to send low-fuel alerts, maintenance reminders and price drops at your favourite stations. It is removed when you turn reminders off or sign out.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data and does not use it for advertising.

**Data Retention Policy**

*   Your account, cars, favourites and reminders: until you delete them or your account.
*   Guest identifiers (no account): deleted after 6 months without use, with their data.
*   Usage log (filters, stations opened, navigation choices): 12 months.
*   Partner clicks: unlinked from you after 12 months; the anonymous count stays for commission reports.
*   Crash reports: up to 90 days at Firebase Crashlytics.
*   IP addresses: 1 hour for abuse protection; the hosting provider's request logs for a few days.
*   Price history is not linked to you.

Deleted data can remain in backups until they expire. To have data deleted that you cannot delete in the Application, contact zupilupiapps@gmail.com.
