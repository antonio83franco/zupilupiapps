**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Tanka app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Antonio Franco (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application shows fuel and EV charging prices from official public sources. To do that, it processes the following information:

*   **Location.** Your device's location is used while you use the Application, to show prices near you. If you turn on automatic mode and connect an OBD adapter, the Application also uses your location while you drive, to find the best-priced station ahead when your tank is low. Location is not used for advertising.
*   **App identifier.** The Application signs you in anonymously with a random identifier, so it can load prices and keep your settings. No name or email is needed.
*   **Account (optional).** If you create an account, your email address is stored so you can sign in and sync your favourites, cars and reminders across devices.
*   **How you use the Application.** Searches, filters, the stations you open and the navigation app you choose are used to learn your preferences (for example your usual fuel). Anonymous usage analytics are only collected if you turn them on in Profile → Privacy.
*   **Vehicle data (optional).** If you connect a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter, the Application reads your car's VIN, odometer, fuel level and fault codes to warn you before you run low and to track maintenance.
*   **Crash reports.** When the Application crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, app version, device model and operating system is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Crash reports contain no user identifier and no location.
*   **IP address.** The servers see your device's IP address with every request. It is used to limit abuse (rate limiting) and appears in the hosting provider's request logs, which are kept for a short time.
*   **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, a push token is stored to send low-fuel alerts, maintenance reminders and price drops at your favourite stations.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Service Provider does not sell your personal data and does not use it for advertising.

Some maintenance links and garage offers are partner links. Partners receive only an anonymous click reference, never your personal data.

**Your Rights and Choices**

*   You can turn location, automatic mode and analytics on or off at any time in Profile → Privacy.
*   You can export all your data from Profile → Privacy → Export my data.
*   You can delete your account and all its data from Profile → Privacy → Delete account and data. This also wipes the data stored on your device.
*   If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have the right to access, correct, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact the Service Provider at zupilupiapps@gmail.com.

**Data Retention Policy**

Your data is kept while you use the Application. Deleting your account removes your account and synced data. Price history is kept for a limited time and is not linked to you. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. If you'd like the Service Provider to delete data you have provided, please contact them at zupilupiapps@gmail.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.
