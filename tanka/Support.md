**Delete your data**

*   In the app: Profile → Privacy → Delete account and data. This deletes your account and synced data on our servers and wipes the data stored on your device.
*   Without the app: email zupilupiapps@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. If you never created an account, uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device; the data on our servers is tied only to a random identifier, not to your name or email.

**Export your data**

Profile → Privacy → Export my data.
